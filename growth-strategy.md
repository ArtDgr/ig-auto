# Growth Strategy — @theitsupportguru

Audience-first growth runbook for the faceless Instagram channel. The account already
posts 5×/day Mon–Fri via Buffer + GitHub Actions; this doc sets the audience, voice,
and content/engagement rules the pipeline is tuned to.

## 1. Audience (priority order)

**Primary — "The IT Professional" (ages 25–45)**
- Help-desk, sysadmin, IT manager, MSP engineer, NOC/SOC analyst, cybersecurity analyst.
- Follows because the channel is credible — they validate it to non-tech coworkers.
- Engage hard: correct details, debate comparisons, save troubleshooting workflows, share fixes to teams.
- The **cybersecurity professional** sub-segment is the sharpest amplifier: they share incident/patch/zero-day content and give authority spillover into business buyers.
- What they want: depth, accuracy, current intel (AI/Copilot, chip-shortage impact on refresh cycles, patch/zero-day), tools, zero fluff.

**Secondary — "The Home User" (busy professional / DIY user, 25–45)**
- Non-IT workers, home-office, small business. Need plain-English fixes, save checklists, skim headlines.
- Still the reach engine (they share), but no longer the editorial target.

## 2. Voice

> "The calm, credible IT colleague in the room next door — occasionally funny."

- **Peer-to-peer authority**: precise, plain-English, no hand-holding. Technical precision over beginner sugar-coating.
- **IT-native humor** (tickets, log dumps, alert fatigue, "have you tried turning it off") — never mocking users; mock the *system*.
- **Unbiased editorial**: no paid promos, free-only deals, balanced takes. This is the trust moat.
- **Helper first, news-reader second.** Always the real playbook: exact menu path, exact command, the "why", the gotcha only hands-on experience teaches.

## 3. Content style (mapped to existing slots)

| Slot | Time | Role | Growth job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card 0 | 06:00 | "Thought" — opinionated take on today's news | Reach via hot takes |
| Card 1 | 12:00 | "Story" — real IT scenario + save-worthy carousel | **Saves** (growth currency) |
| Card 2 | 17:00 | "Debate" — comparison / hot take / poll | Comments + shares |
| Card 3 | 20:00 | "Habit" — routine card | Follows (steady-habits identity) |
| Reel | 15:00 | "Show" — dynamic tech visual + narration | Discovery → new followers |

- **Stories** pivot from consumer scenes to incident post-mortems, 3am alert nights, "how I diagnosed X in 10 minutes."
- **Debates (slot 2 = hero):** sysadmin hot takes — "Ditch the VPN for zero trust?", "Is Copilot worth it for IT?", "RAM prices are spiking — pull the refresh forward or wait?", poll in caption.
- **Saves:** troubleshooting runbooks, hardening checklists. Security carousels promoted to co-primary.
- **News leads:** patch/zero-day/CVE and enterprise AI adoption ahead of gadget launches.
- **Humor:** Friday evening "IT-isms" — alert-burnout satire, IT-department energy. Never mocking users.

### 2026 lens
Chip shortage is a recurring storyline: RAM & anything chip-based is pricier. Bake it into
hardware tips, buying guides, and "upgrade now vs wait" debates.

## 4. Cadence

- **Keep:** Mon–Fri, 5 posts/day (cards 06:00/12:00/17:00/20:00 + reel 15:00 AEST), boost-agent repost 18:30.
- **Day 16+:** 1 weekend reel (Sat) + "Saturday Saves" carousel (Sun) — weekend scroll is uncontested.
- **Weeks 3–4:** test a 2nd reel/day (18:30) 2–3×/week in place of a card.

## 5. 30-day content arc (monthly theme rotation)

- **Week 1 — "Fix It Yourself":** confidence builders. Reels of fixes; save-carousels; story slot = real client scenario; debate slot = "Turn it off and on again — real fix or IT's oldest joke?"
- **Week 2 — "Smart Upgrades 2026":** chip-shortage money storyline. "RAM is getting pricier — what to buy before it spikes." Debates: upgrade now vs wait, DDR4 vs DDR5.
- **Week 3 — "AI in Your Workday":** highest-scoring niche. Stories of real AI adoption; debates on Copilot value; pivot on boost data.
- **Week 4 — "Protect Your Digital Life":** security deep-dive. Hardening checklists; phishing-spotter saves; "password managers worth it?" debate.

### Weekly mix targets (in existing slots)
- Thoughts ~8–9, Stories (save-carousels) ~8, Debates ~4–5 (2 with polls), Humor ~3 (Friday), Habits/routines ~5–6.

## 6. Engagement tactics

1. **Reply to every comment within 60 minutes** of posting (posting slots align with US/EU evenings; reply within the next slot window).
2. **CTA rotation** (never the same ask twice): Save this • "What's your fix?" • "Share with the coworker who needs this" • "A or B? comment".
3. **"You asked, I answered"** — answer the best comment as tomorrow's card; feature the best commenter fix each Friday ("Community Intel").
4. **Pin best-performing reel**; repost winners via boost agent weekly.
5. **Hashtags:** 30–40% niche (#sysadmin #cybersecurity #helpdesk #infosec #windows11 #copilot), rest broad; 8–10 per post.
6. **Engage 10–15 IT/security creators daily** (first 2 weeks) — signal to the in-group.
7. **Faceless Q&A:** static "Ask Me Anything" graphic; answer on next reel/card.
8. **Reshare user wins** ("Tag us when it works"), permission-based.
9. **Cross-post reels** to Facebook/YouTube (config has youtube:0/tiktok:0 — reels are free reach multipliers).
10. **Weekly:** read `data/performance.json`, double down on top niche, retire weakest (boost agent already automates reweighting).

## 7. Follower math

- Baseline: reels at 2–10k reach → ~30–80 new followers/week consistently.
- With 5+ reels/week + save-optimized carousels + reply speed: **+1,000–3,000 followers by day 30** if Week-1 reels sustain 10k+ reach.
- IT-pro audience converts slower but compounds: one respected sysadmin sharing to a team of 40 is worth 10 home users. Expect slower Week-1 growth, faster Week-3+ as authority spreads.
